Hierarchy

Organisational structure, reporting lines, team tiers and role responsibilities across the account delivery model.

Organisational Structure
Account Hierarchy Levels
L1
Account Leadership
Accountable for overall delivery, client relationship, commercial performance and strategic direction of the engagement.
L2
Program / Project Managers
Responsible for planning, scheduling, risk management and cross-team coordination across all workstreams.
L3
Team Leads
Technical and functional leads driving day-to-day delivery, quality standards and team guidance within each domain.
L4
Engineers & Analysts
Core delivery professionals executing development, testing, analysis and operational tasks as defined in sprint and project plans.
L5
Support & Operations
Service desk and operations team managing incidents, service requests, monitoring and day-to-day operational support.
Roles & Responsibilities
L1 — Account Leadership
Owns client relationships, P&L, and executive decision-making
L2 — Program Managers
Manages delivery plans, risk registers, budgets and milestones
L3 — Team Leads
Drives technical direction, code standards and team performance
L4/L5 — Delivery & Ops
Executes delivery tasks, supports operations and handles service requests
Reporting Lines
Direct Reports
Each level reports directly to the tier above in the hierarchy
Matrix Reporting
Functional specialists may report into both a domain lead and a project manager
Escalation Ownership
Escalations follow the hierarchy — L3 → L2 → L1 as required
